Why cash-out windows bounce around

Banners shouting 2–24h rarely mention manual reviews, holidays, busy queues, or your first chunky withdrawal. Upload ID early so you're not stranded when funds suddenly matter.

Crypto versus banks

Crypto adds miner fees and easy mistakes with addresses or memo tags. Banks can feel slower but disputes ride familiar rails—pick what you trust, not whichever keyword sounds futuristic.
