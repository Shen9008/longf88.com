Why withdrawal quotes flex

Ads citing 2–24h rarely mention manual review spikes, public holidays, chain congestion, or first-time large cash-outs. Finish KYC early so emergencies are not stuck behind document workflows.

Crypto versus fiat discipline

On-chain transfers inherit miner fees and memo/tag rules; typos are costly. Fiat rails may feel slower yet dispute pathways differ—pick based on support familiarity, not hype.
