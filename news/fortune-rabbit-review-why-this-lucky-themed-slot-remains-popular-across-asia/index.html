By incorporating cultural elements, the game not only entertains but also enriches players' understanding and appreciation of Asian traditions.

These design choices not only enhance the visuals but also contribute to a player's sense of connection during gameplay. Beyond that, many players cherish games that embody their cultural heritage, making Fortune Rabbit a feast for the eyes as well as a means of celebrating tradition.
