Responsible gambling

18+ only. Betting should stay optional entertainment. If it interferes with finances, relationships, or mental health, pause and seek independent support.

Immediate steps

  • Set deposit, loss, and session caps inside your operator account.
  • Use cooling-off periods before reloading.
  • Talk openly with someone you trust.
